# Anti-Patterns Verdict

Passes first-order slop test, fails second-order: the "technical-forensic dark editorial" lane
(graphite + acid accent + mono annotations) is the guessable second reflex for an AI-engineer
portfolio. Confirmed hits: sentence-case mono eyebrow above 10/10 sections (SectionHeading.jsx);
decorative 01-numbering in 6+ non-sequential contexts (Capabilities, BuildNotes, timeline — where
numbers contradict dates — mobile menu, hero eyebrows); IBM Plex Mono (reflex-reject list) as
costume on ~30 ordinary editorial labels, ~20 of them at 0.48–0.68rem; BuildNotes = three
identical columns; hero scene cards = canonical icon-card anatomy floated in 3D. Clean: no
side-stripes, no gradient text, no hero metrics, no skill bars, no fake links/testimonials.

Deterministic scan: 97 findings, all in frontend/src/index.css — 63 off-system font sizes
(~24 below 0.65rem), 15 off-system radii, 17 off-system colors, 2 font warnings (false
positives: naming drift "Variable" suffix). Line 80 font-size 1rem also FP (clamp lower bound).
Browser evidence: Chrome screenshots at 1440px/390px of dist build; renders correctly.

# Priority Issues

- [P1] Illegible annotation layer: ~20 mono elements at 7.7–10.9px (trace card 0.51rem,
  pipeline 0.54rem, artifact chrome 0.58rem, scan-box 0.48rem). Violates the site's own
  WCAG/200%-zoom commitment. Fix: 0.72rem floor; cull ~half outright.
- [P1] One section grammar ×10 + numbered scaffolding in 6 non-sequential contexts;
  contradicts DESIGN.md's own "one deliberate ordered sequence" rule. Fix: numbers only
  where order is information; ≥3 sections get different opening moves.
- [P1] Four identical project mega-blocks; case study re-serves the same artifact larger.
  Fatigue lands exactly on the proof content; CACE gets lab-equal treatment. Fix: break
  symmetry by importance (CACE flagship, RAG standard, FLAN-T5/PalPath compact).
- [P2] Hero scene decorates more than communicates (orbits + glowing core = AI theatre in
  tasteful costume); restates one sentence three times; 470px of decoration on mobile.
  Fix: make it the actual CACE examination scene or halve it.
- [P2] Fabricated 56%→82% before/after bars in FLAN-T5 artifact with an 8px disclaimer;
  undercuts the site's honesty system. Fix: non-quantified motif.
